Lets check those Landing Pages

The Liine landing page checker scans your landing pages to ensure they're in good standing with Liine's services.

Your uploaded sites will be checked by our automated website scraper. The scraper will check your site for key items to ensure there is no disconnect between the Liine dashboard and your websites. The checker starts by reporting the status of URL redirects, followed by locating all phone numbers on the site. Once all phone numbers have been found they will be cross checked with the Liine tracking numbers database. After verifying the phone numbers, the Liine checker will scan the website for all forms, and document their integration standings. Lastly, your sites will be checked for known booking vendors, and found vendor links will be checked to see if they're properly integrated.

After the checker has finished inspecting all provided pages, a csv report will be emailed to you! Ready to get started? Upload your CSV here!
