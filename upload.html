Landing Page Upload Instructions

Please follow the guidelines below to ensure your landing pages are properly processed:

  • Upload MUST be a CSV file
  • Maximum amount of pages per upload: 500
  • The CSV uploaded should only contain the columns listed:
    1. Column 1: "Landing Page"
    2. Column 2: "Account Name"
    3. Column 3: "CRS ID" (NOTE: "CRS ID" values should only contain digits)
    4. Column 4: "Owner"
    5. Column 5: "Email"

After uploading your CSV, a confirmation message will appear on your screen, and the page will refresh. Please allow up to five minutes for the confirmation to appear. If you do not see this message after waiting, please refresh the page and re upload your csv. If your uplaod was successful, you will receive an email within 1 hour with a csv attachment containing the processed results.
